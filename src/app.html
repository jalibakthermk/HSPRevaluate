Method notes
  • Lamps at ±separation/2, mounting height above the road. Vertical illuminance E = I·cos/d², nearest point of a 0.05° grid (45°L–45°R, 15°D–15°U).
  • Zones A/B: 1, 3, 5 lx on the road along 0, 1.5, 3 m R (B turned 5° R). Zone C: 250 mm, 3, 4.5, 6 m L. Zone D: 3 lx width on the road at 30/40/50 m. Zone E: 3 lx width at 250 mm, 10/20 m.
  • Points A–E: 3 lx along rays to (0, h), (−20, h), (0, h+9), (+20, h), (0, h−2) at 100 m.
  • Glare: weighted flux into x −7.9…+1.3 m, 0.93…1.62 m at 50 m. Flux: LB 15°D–5°U, HB 5°D–10°U, both 45°L–45°R, summed for the lamp pair; each 0.01° cell counts tan²(0.01°) sr as in the reference, or cos V·dH·dV with the cos V option.
  • P = (V − V₁)/(V₂ − V₁) + 1; EPLB = 5.80N + 64.17, EPHB = 11.71N + 141.27, EPADB = 10.87N + 94.13; score = 0.626·MEP − 36.42. Ranks per GTB Table 18.
  • Calculation and rounding as the TU Darmstadt reference software. Differences: Type B converted, Type C supported, candela multiplier applied, unmet lines warned.
